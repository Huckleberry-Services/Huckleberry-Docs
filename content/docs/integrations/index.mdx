Huckleberry integrates with external systems to fit into your existing
workflows &mdash; for sending alerts, syncing data, and powering automations.

## Available integrations

- **[eGauge Meters](/docs/integrations/egauge)** &mdash; connect an eGauge
  energy or water meter and import its interval readings, with history
  backfilled automatically.
- **[Notifications](/docs/integrations/notifications)** &mdash; route alerts
  and report deliveries to Slack and Microsoft Teams channels via webhooks.

More integrations will be documented here as they roll out.
