---
title: eGauge Meters
description: Connect an eGauge energy or water meter and bring its interval readings into Huckleberry, with history backfilled automatically.
---

Huckleberry can read interval data straight from an **eGauge** meter. Connect
one register (one measured circuit or feed) and Huckleberry imports its history,
then keeps polling for new readings. Electricity comes in as kWh per interval
plus average kW. Water comes in by volume and is shown in gallons.

A guided setup walks you through the connection in seven short steps and shows
you a sample of real readings before anything is saved.

<Callout type="info">
**One register becomes one meter.** If an eGauge measures several circuits,
for example the grid feed and a solar array, set up each register you want
as its own connector.
</Callout>

## Before You Start

You'll need:

- **The meter's HTTPS address.** This is usually the eGauge proxy address,
  `https://<device-name>.egauge.io`, or the meter's own public HTTPS
  address. Addresses that only work inside your building's network can't be
  reached by Huckleberry.
- **A username and password** for the meter. A read-only account is enough.
- **The site the meter serves**, already set up in Huckleberry.

## Setting Up a Connector

Go to **Admin &rarr; Data Connectors**, choose **+ New connector**, select
**eGauge meter**, and click **Next**.

![The New connector dialog with the eGauge meter template](/images/integrations/egauge/01-new-connector.png)

### 1. Site

Pick the site the meter serves. Its readings are stored on a meter at this
site.

![Choosing the site](/images/integrations/egauge/02-site.png)

### 2. Connect

Enter the meter's address, username, and password, then choose **Test
connection and discover registers**. Testing saves nothing. The credentials
are stored, encrypted, only when you activate the connector, and they're
never shown again.

![Entering the eGauge address and credentials](/images/integrations/egauge/03-connect.png)

If the test fails, the message tells you why. Common causes:

- **Wrong username or password.** Check the credentials on the meter.
- **Address not reachable.** Use the `https://` proxy address rather than a
  local network address.

### 3. Register

Huckleberry lists every register on the meter. Pick the one to import.

![Picking a register](/images/integrations/egauge/04-register.png)

Huckleberry decides what a register measures from its type on the meter, not
from its name, so a register labelled "Water" that actually measures power is
still treated as electricity. A register that's already imported by another
connector is marked **Already connected**, with a link to that connector.

### 4. Units

This step shows what the register measures and how its readings are stored.

![How the register's readings will be stored](/images/integrations/egauge/05-units.png)

- **Electricity** registers are stored as kWh per interval, plus average kW.
  There's nothing to confirm.
- **Volume** registers can measure water, gas, or other flows. Confirm that the
  register measures **water** to continue. Water is stored by volume and
  shown in gallons. Gas and other volume registers aren't supported yet.

### 5. Solar & Flow

Huckleberry suggests values based on the register. Check them, because they
decide how the meter counts in your totals.

![Choosing solar and flow](/images/integrations/egauge/06-solar-flow.png)

| The register is&hellip; | Solar | Flow |
|---|---|---|
| The grid feed at a site with solar panels | Yes | Net |
| A solar (PV) inverter | Yes | Generation |
| A generator | No | Generation |
| A load or a whole-building feed | No | Consumption |

A **Net** register measures what's drawn from the grid minus what's exported,
so some intervals can be negative. That's expected.

The solar question appears only for electricity.

### 6. Meter

Create a new meter at the site, or attach the register to an existing meter.
Only meters that match the utility, flow, and solar choice, and that don't
already receive readings from somewhere else, are offered. Give the meter and
the connector a name.

![Naming the meter and connector](/images/integrations/egauge/07-meter.png)

### 7. Preview and Activate

Choose the reading **interval** (1 minute, 5 minutes, 15 minutes, or 1 hour)
and how many **days of history** to backfill (up to 730).

Huckleberry runs a set of checks and reads a sample of real intervals from the
meter. Nothing is saved yet. The sample shows the total, the time zone the
meter reports in, how each value was calculated, and any gaps.

![The preview, with checks and sample readings](/images/integrations/egauge/08-preview.png)

When every check passes, choose **Activate**. Huckleberry starts importing the
history right away, and then polls for new readings on the interval you picked.

## Watching the Import

Open the connector from **Data Connectors** to see how the import is going.

![Health and progress for an active eGauge connector](/images/integrations/egauge/09-health.png)

- **Health and progress** shows the connector's status, the last sync, and
  any failures in a row.
- **Backfill and data** shows how far back history goes, how much has been
  imported, and the latest reading stored. The bar fills as the backfill
  runs. A long history is imported over several runs.
- **Gaps** are periods where the meter had no data. **Dropped resets and
  spikes** are intervals Huckleberry left out because the meter's counter
  reset or jumped implausibly. Leaving them out keeps your totals accurate.
- **Source** shows the meter's details, including its clock. A large clock
  skew can make readings land in the wrong interval.

Use **Sync now** to run an import immediately, and **Pause** to stop polling
for a while.

## Changing a Connector

Some settings can be changed at any time: the connector's name, the flow, the
solar choice, and how many days of history to backfill. A longer backfill
reads further back; a shorter one just stops reading older history.

![Fixed setup and the settings you can change](/images/integrations/egauge/10-fixed-setup.png)

The site, meter, register, utility, and interval are fixed, because changing
them would mix readings from different sources. To change any of them, retire
the connector and run the guided setup again.

**Updating credentials.** If the meter's password changes, enter the new
username and password under **Credentials**. They're checked against the same
meter before they replace the old ones.

![Updating credentials and retiring a connector](/images/integrations/egauge/11-credentials-retire.png)

**Retiring a connector** stops its imports permanently and frees the register.
The meter and all of its readings are kept. Retiring can't be undone. To
import the same register again, run the guided setup, which reuses the same
meter.

## How eGauge Readings Show Up

- **Electricity** meters appear with your site's other meters and count toward
  energy totals, charts, and exports according to the flow you chose.
- **Solar** meters are counted as solar generation and are kept separate from
  the energy your buildings use.
- **Water** meters are shown in gallons and are never included in electricity
  totals, kWh charts, or energy exports.
