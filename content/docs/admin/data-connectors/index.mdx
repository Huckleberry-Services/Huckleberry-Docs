---
title: Data Connectors
description: Connect meters and other data sources so Huckleberry imports their readings automatically.
---

Data Connectors bring readings from your meters and other systems into
Huckleberry automatically, so you don't have to export and upload data by hand.
Find them under **Admin &rarr; Data Connectors**.

## Available connectors

- **[eGauge Meters](/docs/admin/data-connectors/egauge)** &mdash; connect an
  eGauge energy or water meter and import its interval readings, with history
  backfilled automatically.

More connectors will be documented here as they roll out.

To send alerts and reports to Slack or Microsoft Teams, see
[Notifications](/docs/admin/account-management/notifications).
