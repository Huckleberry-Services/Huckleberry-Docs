Bill Scanning reads a utility bill for you. Upload the PDF (or a photo or scan)
and Huckleberry extracts the vendor, account number, service period, and the
itemized charges &mdash; then opens the manual entry form with everything filled
in for your review. Instead of typing a bill line by line, you check it.

Find it under **Analytics &rarr; Utility Data &rarr; Utility Entry**, then the
**Upload a Bill (OCR)** button next to Manual Entry.

<Callout type="info">
**Nothing is saved until you submit.** Scanning only pre-fills the form. You
review every field, correct anything the scan got wrong, and the bill is
recorded only when you complete the wizard &mdash; the same way as a manual
entry.
</Callout>

## Scanning a Bill

1. Open **Utility Entry** and choose **Upload a Bill (OCR)**.
2. Upload the bill &mdash; PDF, PNG, JPEG, or TIFF, up to 10&nbsp;MB.
   Multi-page PDFs are fine; all pages are read together.
3. Wait a few seconds while the bill is scanned.
4. The manual entry form opens with the extracted values filled in. Review,
   correct, and submit as usual. The uploaded file is kept with the entry as
   the bill image.

Where a name on the bill matches a vendor or service type you already use,
the form fills in your existing name rather than the bill&rsquo;s spelling, so
your data stays consistent.

## Reviewing What Was Read

Every extracted field carries a colored dot showing how confident the scan is:

- **Green** &mdash; read cleanly; a glance is usually enough.
- **Amber** &mdash; probably right; worth checking against the bill.
- **Red** &mdash; uncertain; check it carefully.

A banner at the top offers **Review next field**, which steps you through the
fields that deserve attention in order. The original bill is displayed
side-by-side with the form, so you can compare without switching windows. Once
you edit a field, its marker clears &mdash; the value is yours, not the
scan&rsquo;s.

**The location is always your choice.** Huckleberry never assigns a bill to a
site on its own. When the account&rsquo;s history or the service address on the
bill points to one of your sites, a suggestion appears under the Location
field &mdash; one click applies it, but only you can make that call.

## Good to Know

- **PDFs with several statements** &mdash; some utilities bundle multiple
  statements in one file. You&rsquo;ll be asked which one to enter; upload the
  file again for the others.
- **Duplicate warning** &mdash; if a bill for the same account and period is
  already in Huckleberry, the form warns you before you submit a second copy.
- **Charges that don&rsquo;t add up** &mdash; if the itemized lines don&rsquo;t
  reach the bill total, the difference appears as a clearly labeled
  reconciliation line for you to resolve.

## If a Bill Won&rsquo;t Scan

- **Password-protected or damaged files** can&rsquo;t be read &mdash;
  you&rsquo;ll see a clear message. Print-to-PDF from the utility&rsquo;s
  portal usually produces a readable copy.
- **Poor-quality photos** scan with lower confidence. A straight-on photo in
  good light, or the original PDF, works best.
- If a document doesn&rsquo;t look like a utility bill, the form says so and
  asks you to check every field.
- Manual entry is always available as a fallback &mdash; a failed scan never
  blocks you from entering the bill by hand.
