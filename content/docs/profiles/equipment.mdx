title: Equipment
description: View and work with equipment across your facilities, including specifications and history.
---

You can view and work with equipment across your facilities, including
specifications and history. Having this information in one place helps
maintenance teams and analysts work from the same data.

- Searchable lists of your equipment.
- Equipment details pulled together from your various data sources.

Huckleberry pulls equipment information from connected building systems (such
as Niagara) as well as other records you maintain.

See [Facility Management](/docs/dashboards/facility-management) for how
equipment appears in live monitoring.
