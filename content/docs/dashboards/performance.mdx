The Performance dashboard is the primary hub for cost, usage, savings, and
performance across all facilities.

Key capabilities:
- Monthly cost/usage trends with multi-utility breakdown (Electric, Gas, Water, Steam).
- Peak demand tracking.
- Cumulative energy savings visualization.
- Performance score calculation and tracking.
- Geographic heat maps and location ranking/comparison.
- Customizable, user-persistent chart layouts and saved filters.
- Prior-period comparisons with normalized "apples-to-apples" logic.

## Your home dashboard

Your personalized landing page. Follow specific sites and see aggregated views
without leaving the homepage.

- Global site filter (all followed sites or single-site drill-down).
- Widgets for Utility Costs (stacked bars), Recent Invoices and Equipment Status (with a 30-minute freshness indicator).
- Drag-and-drop reordering, with configurable sizing (half or full width, row height).
- Your layout, followed sites and widgets are saved to your account, so they follow you between devices.
- Equipment detail windows with live readings.
- Administrators can manage dashboards for their organization.

See also [Comparison](/docs/analytics/comparison) for ranking sites, and
[Sites](/docs/profiles/sites) for site master data.
