---
title: Facility Management
description: Live building monitoring — equipment status, sensor readings, fault detection and alarms.
---

Facility Management is the live view of your buildings, combining data from
your connected building systems.

- Live equipment status and sensor readings.
- Fault detection (custom FDD rules) and building-system alarms.
- Advanced FDD features: cause-and-effect narratives per rule, per-detector evidence, historical context for open faults, and an alarm lifecycle (active, acknowledged, resolved) with batch decisions.
- Alarm events table with severity, status, breach direction, filtering, and search.
- Site detail pages with images, coordinates and address.
- Direct access to your building controls from inside Huckleberry (see [Facility Controls](/docs/dashboards/facility-controls)).
