title: Facility Management
description: Live building monitoring — equipment status, sensor readings, fault detection and alarms.
---

Facility Management is the live view of your buildings, combining data from
your connected building systems.

- Live equipment status and sensor readings.
- Fault detection (custom FDD rules) and building-system alarms.
- Advanced FDD features: cause-and-effect narratives per rule, per-detector evidence, historical context for open faults, and an alarm lifecycle (active, acknowledged, resolved) with batch decisions.
- Alarm events table with severity, status, breach direction, filtering, and search.
- Site detail pages with images, coordinates and address.
- Each piece of equipment can open its own controls graphic from your building system, inside Huckleberry (see [Equipment Graphics](/docs/dashboards/facility-management/equipment-graphics)).

## Your portfolio at a glance

The top of the page sums up every site, then lists them one by one.

![Facility Management overview: headline figures and the list of sites and buildings](/images/facility-management/01-overview.png)

- **Headline figures** &mdash; active alarms, open faults with their
  estimated cost so far, how much equipment is online, current demand,
  energy used today and when the latest reading arrived.
- **Sites** &mdash; each site with its photo and address. Sites with
  connected buildings show, per building, how much equipment is online,
  demand right now, energy today compared with yesterday, energy per square
  foot and any open faults. The sparkline shows energy use over the last
  seven days.
- **View Options** &mdash; switch between the list and a grid of site cards.

Select a site or building name to open its [detail page](#a-site).

## Meters

Every connected meter, its latest demand, today's energy (or water) and
when it last reported.

![The Meters table: latest demand, today's energy and status for each meter](/images/facility-management/02-meters.png)

A meter is **Reporting** when its readings are up to date and **Stale**
when they've stopped arriving. Sub-meters that sit under a parent meter are
marked **excluded · in parent meter**, so their energy isn't counted twice.

## Needs attention and faults

![Needs attention, and faults across the portfolio with their impact so far](/images/facility-management/03-attention-faults.png)

- **Needs attention** &mdash; equipment with an open fault, with when it
  last sent data.
- **Faults across portfolio** &mdash; open faults by site: how many, their
  estimated cost so far, the most common fault types and what's already
  being handled, such as a repair being verified or a fault clearing. Filter
  by severity with **High**, **Medium** and **Low**.

Cost estimates are a range. Where a unit's size (tonnage) isn't recorded,
Huckleberry assumes a typical size and says so. Add the tonnage to the
equipment record to narrow the range.

## Fault detection coverage

How much of your equipment fault detection is actually watching, and how
good the data feeding it is.

![FDD coverage: share of equipment watched, data quality score, detectors and sites](/images/facility-management/04-fdd-coverage.png)

- **Watched by fault detection** &mdash; the share of your equipment,
  weighted by size, that has fault detection running on it.
- **Data quality score** &mdash; how complete and current the readings
  behind fault detection have been over the last 30 days.
- **Not watched** &mdash; why some units aren't covered, for example
  equipment that isn't linked to a building controller yet.
- **By detector** and **By site** &mdash; where coverage is strong and
  where it's thin, least-watched sites first.

A **Units needing attention first** list below names the specific units to
fix.

## Alarms

Building-system alarms, newest first.

![The Alarms table with point, equipment, type, severity, value against threshold and status](/images/facility-management/05-alarms.png)

Each alarm shows the point and equipment it came from, whether it went over
a high limit or under a low one, its severity, the reading against the
threshold, and its status: **active**, **acknowledged** or **resolved**.
Filter by date range and status, search by point or equipment, and print,
copy or export the table.

## A site

A site's page brings together its energy use, floor plans, faults and
alarms. Choose **All buildings** or a single building.

![A site page: the filters for performance period and meters](/images/facility-management/06-site-filters.png)

Use **Filters** to pick the performance period and the meters to include,
or tick **Use latest available 30 days**. **Export to Excel** downloads the
data behind the page.

### Heat map and electric meters

![The site heat map with demand statistics, and the electric meter chart compared with an earlier week](/images/facility-management/07-heat-map-meters.png)

- **Heat Map** &mdash; demand for every hour of every day in the period,
  from green (low) to red (high). Blue cells mean the site was exporting
  power, for example from solar. Filter by day of the week. Below the map:
  energy used, peak, average and lowest demand, load factor, the share used
  outside business hours, weekday against weekend demand and when the site
  usually peaks.
- **Electric Meters** &mdash; demand over the period. Tick **Add
  Comparison** to overlay an earlier period and shade the savings between
  them, with the difference charted below and a **Comparison Summary** of
  energy, average and peak demand.

### Floor plans

![A floor plan with live sensor readings in each room](/images/facility-management/08-floor-plan.png)

Pick a building and floor to see equipment and sensors where they sit, with
their live readings. Pins are coloured by status: live, fault, down, alarm,
stale, overridden, disabled or no data. Select a pin or an equipment symbol
for details. Zoom with Ctrl and scroll (or pinch), drag to pan, and use
**Full screen** for a closer look.

### Faults

![A site's open faults with severity, impact so far and status](/images/facility-management/09-site-faults.png)

The site's faults, with severity, estimated cost so far, status and how long
each has been open. Select a fault to see what it means and where it stands.

![A fault's detail: what it means, its repair progress and its impact so far](/images/facility-management/10-fault-detail.png)

The detail explains the fault in plain language, including the usual causes.
It also shows progress: once a repair is recorded, the fault closes on its
own after enough clean checks, and starts over if the problem comes back.

The site page also has its own **FDD coverage**, a utility drift check
against weather-normalized usage, and the site's **Alarms**.
