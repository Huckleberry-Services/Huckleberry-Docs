---
title: Environmental Footprint
description: Carbon emissions and water usage analytics, with goals, trends and branded reports.
---

Environmental Footprint covers carbon emissions and water usage, with
sustainability tracking. It has two views: **Water Footprint** and
**Carbon Footprint**.

- Annual + trailing-12-month trends (dual-axis tons + tons/sqft).
- KPIs with prior-period arrows.
- Goal tracking (base year vs current with performance line).
- Month-aligned normalization for fair comparisons.
- Unit-aware conversions (CCF, CF, gallons, etc.) with automatic base-year detection.
- Invoice anomaly detection (with sensitivity control) that automatically excludes outliers from totals.
- Missing-data banners for sites excluded from comparisons.
- Top performers (efficiency and reduction) with medal badges.
- Branded PDF exports for carbon and water (charts + KPIs + tables).
- Boosters & detractors reference tables.
