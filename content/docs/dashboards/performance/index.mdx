The Performance dashboard is the primary hub for cost, usage, savings, and
performance across all facilities.

Key capabilities:
- Monthly cost/usage trends with multi-utility breakdown (Electric, Gas, Water, Steam).
- Peak demand tracking.
- Cumulative energy savings visualization.
- Performance score calculation and tracking.
- Geographic heat maps and location ranking/comparison.
- Customizable, user-persistent chart layouts and saved filters.
- Prior-period comparisons with normalized "apples-to-apples" logic.

## Goals

The top of the dashboard tracks your organization's sustainability goals.

![Long-term goal gauges and this year's carbon, water and waste progress](/images/performance/01-goals.png)

- **Long Term Goal and Performance** &mdash; a gauge for each long-term target,
  such as carbon neutrality or zero waste to landfill, showing how far along
  you are.
- **Goal and Progress** &mdash; this year's carbon savings, water savings and
  waste diversion, each measured against its target.

## At a glance

A quick read on the latest month whose bills are in, compared with an earlier
period.

![At a glance: spend, electricity, natural gas and sites to review for the latest month](/images/performance/02-at-a-glance.png)

- **Compare cards to** &mdash; switch between the prior month, prior quarter,
  prior year or your comparison period.
- **Spend, Electricity and Natural gas** &mdash; the current total, the
  comparison figure and the change. **Coverage details** shows which sites
  had complete bills for both periods.
- **Sites to review** &mdash; sites where energy cost rose more than 2% while
  usage of the same fuel didn't. These are worth a closer look for rate or
  billing changes.

## Sites by period

Every site side by side, ranked by how much its spend changed.

![Sites by period: each site's spend change against the prior month, quarter and year](/images/performance/03-sites-by-period.png)

Each cell is colour-coded: red for an increase of more than 2%, green for a
decrease of more than 2%, and neutral for changes within 2%. Cells also flag
bills that are still loading or months with nothing billed. Use **Needs
review** and **Needs data** to narrow the list, switch the usage columns
between electricity and gas, or search for a site.

## Filters

Select **show filters** to choose what the charts below cover.

![Performance and saving filters: periods, locations, service types and chart options](/images/performance/04-filters.png)

- **Performance Period** and **Comparison Period** &mdash; the months being
  analysed and the baseline they're measured against.
- **Locations** &mdash; filter by facility, state or group.
- **Service types** &mdash; electricity, gas, water and so on.
- **Comparison line on charts** &mdash; overlay the comparison period on the
  charts.
- **Weather Normalization** &mdash; adjust for differences in weather between
  the two periods, for a fairer comparison.
- **Milestone Markers** &mdash; show the milestones recorded for your sites,
  such as an equipment upgrade, on the charts.

Select **Apply Filters** to update the dashboard. Your filters are saved, so
they're still there next time you sign in.

## Map and performance score

![The performance map and the performance score bubble chart](/images/performance/05-map-score.png)

- **Performance Map** &mdash; each facility on a map. Colour shows the chosen
  measure, for example electricity per square foot, and circle size shows the
  facility's total. **Export** downloads the data.
- **Performance Score** &mdash; plots each facility's total against its
  intensity (its value per square foot), so outliers stand out. Bubble size
  represents facility area. Switch to **Ranked sites** to list facilities
  from the highest value per square foot down &mdash; the quickest way to see
  which buildings use the most energy per square foot.

## Cost and savings

![Monthly total utility cost by service, and cumulative monthly energy savings](/images/performance/06-cost-savings.png)

- **Monthly Total Utility Cost** &mdash; cost per month, stacked by service.
  Tick **Show predicted** to fill in an estimate for months whose bills
  haven't arrived yet, shown separately from billed amounts.
- **Cumulative Monthly Energy $ Savings** &mdash; savings against the
  comparison period, adding up month by month.

Drag the slider under either chart to zoom into a range of months. **Savings
data details** explains which sites and months are included.

## Consumption and savings

Compares the performance period with the comparison period, site by site
against each site's own bills for the same months.

![Total cost comparison: performance period, comparison period and the savings between them](/images/performance/07-consumption-savings.png)

**Total Cost Comparison** shows the cost of each period by service and the
savings or loss between them. **Total Carbon Comparison** does the same for
emissions.
