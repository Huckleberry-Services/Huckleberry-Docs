---
title: My Dashboard
description: Build a personal homepage with the sites and widgets you care about.
---

My Dashboard is a personal page that shows the sites you follow and the
widgets you choose, such as utility costs, recent invoices and equipment
status. You can make it the first page you see when you sign in.

Your dashboard belongs to you. Your followed sites, widgets and layout are
saved to your account, so they follow you from device to device and don't
change anyone else's view. My Dashboard is available to administrator
accounts.

## Set it up

### 1. Turn it on

Select your name at the top right, then **Profile**. Under
**Dashboard Preferences**, tick **Enable Custom Dashboard**. You may also see
a **New: Build a custom dashboard** banner at the top of the Performance
dashboard, which takes you straight to this setting.

![Dashboard Preferences on the Profile page, with Enable Custom Dashboard ticked](/images/my-dashboard/01-enable.png)

**My Dashboard** now appears at the top of the **Dashboards** menu.

![The Dashboards menu with My Dashboard at the top](/images/my-dashboard/02-sidebar.png)

### 2. Choose your sites

The first time you open My Dashboard, a short guide explains the three steps.
Select **Choose sites to get started**.

![The Set up your dashboard guide: follow your sites, choose widgets, make it your homepage](/images/my-dashboard/03-setup-guide.png)

Tick the sites you want to keep an eye on, then select **Add**. Use the
search box to find a site quickly.

![The Add Sites to Dashboard window with four sites ticked](/images/my-dashboard/04-add-sites.png)

Your dashboard opens with three widgets: **Utility Costs**, **Recent
Invoices** and **Equipment Status**. Each one shows data for your followed
sites only.

### 3. Choose and arrange widgets

Select the gear icon at the top right to choose which widgets appear.
Tick a widget to show it and untick it to hide it.

![The widget picker open above the Utility Costs widget](/images/my-dashboard/05-customize.png)

| Widget | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Utility Costs** | Monthly utility cost by service. |
| **Recent Invoices** | The latest bills for your sites. |
| **Equipment Status** | Your sites' equipment and whether each piece is reporting. |
| **Facility KPIs** | Active alarms, open faults, current demand and energy used today. |
| **Power Meters** | Your connected power meters. |
| **Needs Attention** | Equipment and sites that need a look. |
| **Portfolio Faults** | Open equipment faults across your sites. |
| **Alarms** | Building-system alarms. |

Facility KPIs, Power Meters, Needs Attention, Portfolio Faults and Alarms
use data from your connected building systems. See
[Facility Management](/docs/dashboards/facility-management).

To arrange your widgets:

- **Reorder** &mdash; drag a widget by the handle (⠿) next to its title.
- **Resize** &mdash; drag a widget's bottom-right corner to switch it between
  half and full width. Put two half-width widgets side by side.
- **Collapse** &mdash; select the arrow at the top right of a widget to fold
  it away.
- **Go deeper** &mdash; select a widget's title, **View Details** or
  **View All** to open the full page behind it.

Changes save as you make them.

### 4. Make it your homepage

Tick **Make this my homepage** at the top of the page. From then on you'll
land on My Dashboard every time you sign in.

## Using your dashboard

### Focus on one site

Pick a site from **Filter by site** to show only that site's data in every
widget. Choose **All Sites** to go back to all your followed sites.

![My Dashboard focused on one site, with Augusta Corporate Center selected under Filter by site](/images/my-dashboard/06-focus-site.png)

### Add or remove sites

Select **Add Site** to follow more sites. To stop following a site, select
the **×** on its name, then select it again to confirm.

### Check equipment

**Equipment Status** lists the equipment at your sites. A green dot and
**Connected** mean the equipment has sent a reading in the last 30 minutes.

![The Equipment Status widget listing a site's air handlers, chiller, rooftop unit and meters](/images/my-dashboard/07-equipment-status.png)

Select a piece of equipment to see its latest readings, such as
temperatures, setpoints, fan status and mode. **View Trends** opens its
history.

![An equipment detail window with the latest readings for a rooftop unit](/images/my-dashboard/08-equipment-detail.png)

## Turning it off

Untick **Enable Custom Dashboard** on your Profile to hide My Dashboard.
Your sites and layout are kept, so it's all there if you turn it back on.
