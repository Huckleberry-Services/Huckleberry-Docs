title: Comparison
description: Rank and compare sites across your portfolio by cost, usage, carbon and more.
---

Comparison is for portfolio-level ranking and side-by-side analysis. It makes
it simple to see which locations are performing well and which may need
attention.

## Rank & Sort

- Rank sites by cost, usage, carbon, etc.
- Custom groupings (by group, state, or none).
- Configurable date ranges (performance vs comparison periods), so the
  analysis reflects real operating conditions.
- Stacked bar visualizations.

See [Performance](/docs/dashboards/performance) for trends and heat maps.
