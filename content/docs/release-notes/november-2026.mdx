---
title: November 2026
description: Set up a whole eGauge panel in one pass, group circuits by category, see them in the new Equipment Energy Monitor, no more double-counted circuits, and backfills that never pause live readings.
---

November brings circuit-level energy into Huckleberry: connect a panel's
circuits in one go, group them the way you think about your equipment, and see
where a site's energy goes on its Facility Management page.

**Equipment Energy Monitor**  
Sites with eGauge circuits now have an Equipment Energy Monitor on their
Facility Management page. See today, yesterday, the last 7 or 30 days, or a
custom range, in kWh or kW, with one line per category &mdash; Chillers,
Lighting, Headhouse Climate, whatever you call them. Expand a category to see
its circuits, spot one that has gone offline, and download the table as CSV.
[Learn more](/docs/admin/data-connectors/egauge#equipment-energy-monitor).

**Set Up a Whole Panel at Once**  
The eGauge setup now lets you tick several registers and set them up together:
check each circuit's details in one table, preview them all, and activate them
in one go. If one has a problem, the rest still go ahead and you can retry just
that one. [Learn more](/docs/admin/data-connectors/egauge#setting-up-several-circuits-at-once).

**Group Circuits by Category**  
Give each circuit a category during setup, or change it later in the
connector's settings. Huckleberry suggests one from the register's name and
remembers the categories you've used, so a panel's circuits group consistently.

**No More Double-Counted Circuits**  
A circuit can now be marked as a sub-meter of the site's utility meter, and
setup pre-selects that meter for you. Site totals count the utility meter once,
while each circuit is still shown on its own. A panel's own total register is
recognised too, and kept as a reference rather than added on top of its
circuits. [Learn more](/docs/admin/data-connectors/egauge#sub-meters).

**Circuits That Count Backwards, Fixed Automatically**  
Some eGauge installs record a circuit's usage as a negative number. Huckleberry
now spots this during setup and flips the sign, so those circuits import
correctly instead of being skipped.

**Longer Backfills Without the Wait**  
Extending how far back a connector imports no longer pauses its live readings.
New data keeps arriving while the older history fills in over the next few
runs.
