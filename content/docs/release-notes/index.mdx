## Releases

- **[November 2026](/docs/release-notes/november-2026)** &mdash; the Equipment Energy Monitor, setting up a whole eGauge panel at once, circuit categories, no more double-counted circuits, circuits that count backwards fixed automatically, and longer backfills without the wait.
- **[August 2026](/docs/release-notes/august-2026)** &mdash; verify a package of measures in one project, your own data as an M&amp;V variable, more accurate carbon figures, ranking by carbon and demand, equipment graphics in place, a searchable fault dictionary, and faster invoice lists.
- **[July 2026](/docs/release-notes/july-2026)** &mdash; personalized dashboards, Facility Management as an operations home, more accurate facility metrics, clearer equipment diagnostics, one-click Slack notifications, and automated report delivery.

For earlier updates, previous releases delivered automated collection of utility bills from major providers, comprehensive environmental footprint reports with easy-to-read charts and exports, foundations for personalized views, and overall improvements in platform speed and reliability.
